ONE DECISION / TWO STRUCTURES

Where does the income come from?

Both options preserve upgrades, recruitment, conductor tap income and direct tap damage.

Current recommendation · not accepted

A · Separate mining

Resource site ↓ Dedicated producer ↓ Spendable income ↓ Production / instruments / conductor

What it gives us

Economic locations to reach and protect. Buying more production and buying stronger defense have different consequences.

What it costs

Another entity to place and develop; another demand on limited resources and arena space.

Musical possibility

A strange being draws stored vibration from the landscape on the global beat. It contributes a pulse without becoming a defensive instrument.

Earlier alternative · not accepted

B · Orchestra production

Recruit a being ↓ Place it in the orchestra ↓ Playing produces income ↓ Instruments / conductor / expression

What it gives us

Fewer entity types and a direct relationship between a growing orchestra and growing income.

What it costs

Recruitment can become the answer to both income and defense. Losing a performer can reduce both at once.

Musical possibility

Coordinated playing produces Resonance; dedicated income techniques would need to remain distinct from combat improvements.

Recruitment stays distinct in both. Discovering a being makes a voice available. Arrangement places it. Development changes how it performs. Neither alternative settles what survives defeat.
