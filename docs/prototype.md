## Question under test

Does always-on tap income plus beat- and pitch-relative development make mining, conducting, and tower investment feel like one musical incremental system while preserving each instrument's identity?

## Playable evidence

- Every free-play tap produces Resonance.
- A tap close to the global beat earns one additional capped timing bonus per beat.
- The same tap can accent a nearby mine or instrument by spending conductor power; empty-space tapping does not spend power.
- Touch yield has a small incremental development path.
- Raising global tempo accelerates mining and the orchestra's shared clock.
- Instruments retain stable identities while developing through technique mastery, rhythmic subdivision, and octave doubling.
- Subdivision changes attack cadence against the global beat.
- Octave doubling adds audible pitches in the same instrument voice and increases its attack.
- Recruiting an additional instrument remains a separate positional investment.
- Offline time advances mining without advancing danger.
