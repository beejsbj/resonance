# Six reference games: production, recruitment, and progression

Researched 2026-09-11 for the live orchestra-defense wayfinding discussion. This document records evidence and proposals; it does not settle the economy or amend the product brief. It revisits the earlier proposal in [Genre foundations](genre-foundations.md) that the active orchestra should be the passive-income source.

## What the references support

**Separate mining has a useful job:** create an economic investment and a geographic reason to expand that are distinct from obtaining another defender. Idle progression requires a compounding production loop, but it does not require combat units to be its generators. Recruitment can put discovered beings into inventory while gathering sites continue producing spendable resources.

The sources establish different precedents, not one interchangeable genre formula:

| Reference | Verified production and spending | Distinction useful to Resonance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Space Game**, original 2009 CasualCollective game | The original developer upload describes operating a mining company in asteroid fields while defending against pirate swarms. Its surviving description does not document exact construction prices or network rules. | Economic locations give defense an external purpose: protect an expanding operation. |
| **Cell to Singularity** | Taps earn Entropy; the evolution and civilization trees contain currency-funded progression. The newer Reality Engine adds upgrades affecting production, generator diversity, and prestige conversion. | Unlocking new content, increasing generator output, and improving subsequent progression can be separate rewards. |
| **Mad For Dance – Taptap Dance** | Taps produce Beats; Beats upgrade the DJ and club; improving DJ skill brings more Clubbers. | A musical fiction comfortably accommodates currency, upgrades, and character collection. Music does not require collapsing these into one operation. |
| **Vampire Survivors** | Gold gathered during runs buys upgrades for subsequent survivors; weapon leveling is another progression activity. Its developer identifies single-stick movement and automatic attacks as the combat foundation. | Immediate combat development and lasting upgrades operate on different timescales. Automatic attacks do not make the game an offline idle economy. |
| **Infinitode 2** | Miners extract map resources during defense. Resources and Green Papers fund global research for towers, miners, abilities, and other systems. | Dedicated mining can be strategically valuable without being recruitment or the same budget as immediate fighting strength. |
| **AdVenture Capitalist** | Businesses generate cash; managers automate them, including offline earnings. Angel Investors improve businesses; normally claiming Angels resets progress. | The idle foundation is reinvestment, automation, and escalating output. It works independently of combat or finding characters. |

## Evidence by game

### The Space Game

The relevant title is the original **The Space Game**, not **The Space Game: Missions** and not **Ascent – The Space Game**. Newgrounds preserves the original entry, its 2009 date, CasualCollective credit, and Author Comments. Those comments explicitly quote a Jay Is Games review describing mining asteroids under pirate attack. This is developer-selected description retained at the original upload, rather than an independently observed playthrough. [Original upload record](https://www.newgrounds.com/portal/rip/483463?review-page=121).

The developer's separate *Missions* upload identifies itself as a sequel, with mining, repairing stations, defending jump ships, and destroying enemy ships among its objectives. This confirms the distinction between titles; it is not evidence that every sequel rule existed in the original. [CasualCollective's Missions listing](https://www.kongregate.com/en/games/casualcollective/the-space-game-missions).

**Limit:** exact mineral spending, energy generation, relay behavior, depletion, and mission persistence need original gameplay/tutorial evidence before copying them as rules. A proximity network in Resonance is already the user's direction; this research does not need to manufacture a precise historical rule to justify it.

### Cell to Singularity

Computer Lunch's store description establishes Entropy earned through taps, currency-funded animal evolution, and Ideas spent on civilization technology. The official FAQ describes saving and spending points as the main strategic activity and explains the older simulation-reboot model. [Developer store description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/977400/), [official FAQ](https://celltosingularity.com/faq/).

The FAQ is not a safe source for the exact current prestige economy: the **November 18, 2025 Reality Reboot** announcement introduces distinct Metabits and Knowledge conversions, new Reality Engine upgrades, Research XP and Research Levels, and Research Chips that improve Entropy or Idea production. It also explicitly describes upgrades rewarding generator diversity and biome productivity. This is stronger evidence for production investment than a vague assertion that discovering life is itself income. [Computer Lunch's release announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appgroupname=Cell+to+Singularity+-+Evolution+Never+Ends&appids=977400&enddate=1763755985&feed=steam_community_announcements).

**Interpretation:** take the relationship between generators, upgrade trees, and discovery. Do not import its whole collection of currencies or treat evolution unlocks as identical to recruiting deployable defenders.

### Mad For Dance – Taptap Dance

Birdletter's own instructions are unusually direct: tap for Beats, spend Beats on the DJ and club, and invite Clubbers and celebrities. The description links improved DJ skill to additional Clubbers and presents changing musical eras and a growing club as visible rewards. [Birdletter's Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.birdletter.mfd), [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mad-for-dance-taptap-dance/id1438603283).

**Evidence gap:** secondary guides and store user reviews describe cocktails as passive producers and DJ upgrades as improving tap yield. The publisher-written description does not establish that exact split. Three inspected official Play images were promotional club/character images, not a readable cocktail-production tutorial. Cocktail output, offline caps, recruitment conditions, and reset rules are therefore not treated as verified mechanics here. The store's “Offline” category alone would not establish earnings while the app is closed.

**Interpretation:** the verified precedent supports separate numerical advancement and character collection inside a musical setting. It does not prove Resonance's defenders should generate passive income.

### Vampire Survivors

Poncle's store description separates gold-funded benefits for future survivors from developing offensive weapons during play. The developer's AMA describes the original combat foundation as movement with automatic attacks. [Developer store description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1794680/Vampire_Survivors/), [poncle developer AMA](https://www.reddit.com/r/pcgaming/comments/16rsuh0/).

Poncle's Adventures FAQ gives a particularly explicit example of different progression scopes: buying a weapon can equip it for the current run while unlocking it for later level-up choices throughout that Adventure. Adventures have their own reset/remix structure, so those specific rules should not be generalized to every main-game system. [Official Adventures FAQ](https://poncle.games/adventures-faq).

**Interpretation:** borrow escalating threats, automatic attack combinations, and a distinction between a run's build and lasting unlocks. Conductor tap attacks are Resonance's additional active layer; they are not a Vampire Survivors requirement. This reference does not justify offline combat or enemy kills as the sole income source.

### Infinitode 2

Prineside's press kit explicitly describes resource deposits in levels, miners placed to extract them while defending against waves, and a global research tree paid for with Resources and Green Papers. Research improves towers, miners, abilities, and other systems. Nearby modifiers also change buildings. The official home page explicitly calls these research improvements permanent. [Developer press kit](https://infinitode.prineside.com/?m=press_kit), [official game overview](https://infinitode.prineside.com/).

Update 1.9.0 documents per-tower coin statistics and abilities affecting the mining/combat relationship: Overload increases mining speed and enemy toughness, while Loop boosts both tower damage and extracted resources. These are concrete examples of taking combat risk for economic gain, not just thematic similarity. [Official update notes](https://blog.infinitode.prineside.com/2024/07/infinitode-2-major-update-190-and.html).

**Interpretation:** this is the clearest primary-source support for retaining a dedicated mining domain. However, copying mined resources as the sole currency for immediate construction would not be faithfully copying the documented global-research role. Resonance must choose what its mining output pays for.

### AdVenture Capitalist

Hyper Hippo's store page explicitly describes businesses, cash, Angel Investors, managers, and money accumulating offline. Its support article distinguishes normal progress-resetting Angel claims from the premium method that claims without resetting. [Developer store description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/346900/AdVenture_Capitalist/), [official Angel-claim explanation](https://screenzilla.helpshift.com/hc/en/5-adventure-capitalist/faq/33-why-would-i-use-gold-to-claim-angels/?f=transfer-or-restore-purchases&s=purchases).

Anthony Pecorella, who identifies himself as AdVenture Capitalist's mobile lead producer, explains the broader generator model: production is reinvested in generators with rising costs and milestone multipliers; prestige provides another progression layer. This is design analysis from a producer, not documentation that every formula applies unchanged to every current edition. [The Math of Idle Games, Part I](https://www.kongregate.com/en/pages/the-math-of-idle-games-part-i).

**Interpretation:** a distinct mining system can supply this reinvestment loop. A new gathering site, more capacity at an existing site, and a production multiplier need not correspond to recruiting another instrument.

## Proposed Resonance synthesis

Retain **gathering, recruitment, arrangement, and development as four related activities**. This proposal replaces the earlier recommendation that every active instrument should inherently be an income generator; it awaits the user's decision.

1. **Gathering:** build or awaken dedicated producers at world sites. They generate the ordinary spendable resource, with production upgrades and safe offline accumulation. Their fiction can remain strange: drawing stored vibration out of a sleeping landscape does not require realistic mining equipment.
2. **Recruitment:** discover and awaken instrument beings, adding them to inventory. Reaching a site can expose both a resource opportunity and a potential recruit, but neither reward must be mandatory at every site. Their relationship is still a live decision.
3. **Arrangement:** deploy available beings freely and propagate the conductor's pulse through proximity. Placement decisions concern musical relationships and defense rather than permanently binding a performer to its discovery location.
4. **Development:** spend income on distinguishable improvements to production, automatic defense, and conductor powers. Economic investment competes with surviving the next wave. Preserve conductor taps as both ordinary income and direct attacks, as already confirmed.

This produces two reasons to extend the network: **reach more production and discover more voices**. The orchestra protects expansion; expansion funds development. Musical output can express both gathering and fighting without forcing them to have identical economic roles.

**Cost of the proposal:** another entity class and another placement concern. It earns that complexity only if players make meaningful choices between production and defense. Do not introduce a separate energy currency, five resource types, prestige currencies, or mandatory specialist mining beings merely to resemble the references. First specify what the ordinary resource buys and whether pulse connection is required for gathering. Run losses and persistence remain on the live wayfinding map, not settled by this comparison.
