# Genre foundations for Resonance

Researched 2026-09-11. Evidence for the live orchestra-defense decision; recommendations below are proposals, not settled product decisions. This is a comparison of relevant precedents, not a claim that every game in these genres follows one formula.

Follow-up: Burooj asked to reconsider mining as an independent system against six named references. See [the six-game comparison](six-game-economy-comparison.md). The orchestra-as-primary-income proposal below was not accepted and should not be treated as the current design direction.

## What the precedents establish

| Reference | Verified mechanic | Relevance to Resonance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Kongregate: The Math of Idle Games, Part I](https://www.kongregate.com/en/pages/the-math-of-idle-games-part-i), by Anthony Pecorella, lead producer of mobile AdVenture Capitalist | Generators produce income; purchases increase production; costs and multipliers create changing investment priorities. A generator can produce damage that converts into gold through kills. Prestige resets much of the current economy for persistent gains. | An incremental economy is a production-and-reinvestment loop, not necessarily a mining simulation. Income still has a purpose when recruitment changes. |
| [Clicker Heroes, official site](https://www.clickerheroes.com/) | Clicking damages monsters; kills yield gold; gold hires and upgrades heroes; heroes earn rewards automatically, including while the game is closed. | Direct player attacks, automatic combat, upgrades and idle accumulation are compatible precedents. |
| [Clicker Heroes, developer guide](https://blog.clickerheroes.com/what-are-heroes-and-gilded-heroes-in-clicker-heroes/) | Cid upgrades click damage, while other hired heroes provide automatic damage. | Active attack strength and automatic orchestra strength can have separate development paths. |
| [Infinitode 2, developer's Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/937310/Infinitode_2__Infinite_Tower_Defense/) | Towers defend a base against waves, have different enemy matchups, gain experience and receive upgrades. Neighboring modifiers affect towers or mining. Mined resources fund research; research can also improve miners. | Defense, economic growth, local relationships and broader progression can remain distinct even when tightly connected. |
| [Infinitode 2, official 1.9.0 update notes](https://blog.infinitode.prineside.com/2024/07/infinitode-2-major-update-190-and.html) | Overload increases both mining speed and enemy toughness. Loop boosts damage and mined resources. | The developer explicitly couples economic opportunities with combat conditions; this supports an economy-versus-safety decision, without requiring Resonance to copy those abilities. |
| [The Space Game: Missions, CasualCollective's upload](https://www.kongregate.com/en/games/casualcollective/the-space-game-missions) | The developer's description names repairing stations, defending jump ships, mining asteroids and destroying enemy ships. | This is evidence for multiple economic and defensive objectives. The description does **not** establish exact relay behavior or upgrade costs. |
| [Vampire Survivors, developer's Steam description](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1794680/Vampire_Survivors/) | Survival runs end in death; collected gold buys upgrades benefiting subsequent survivors. The starting advice distinguishes leveling offensive weapons from buying power-ups. | A survival run can have immediate build development and a longer progression layer. This does not settle what Resonance should retain after defeat. |

The Space Game source above is the sequel, not a verified rules reference for the original. Only its developer description was used; the portal FAQ and player comments were not treated as first-party mechanics documentation. Exact original-game proximity rules need further primary evidence if they become decisive.

## Recommended foundation

Keep the conventional economic loop: **produce Resonance → invest → increase production or fighting strength → survive and reach farther**. Music supplies the activity, behavior and feedback of that loop. It does not require replacing currency with a novel system.

1. **Give the active orchestra passive Resonance production.** Let coordinated playing earn a measurable amount per second, including during safe offline progress. This is a proposed musical equivalent of an idle generator. It does not require audible music or simulated combat while the app is closed. Enemy rewards can later supplement this; making kills the only income source would conflict with the already agreed separation between offline production and danger unless offline combat were simulated abstractly.
2. **Keep taps as both income and direct combat.** Every ordinary tap retains its base income; a tap can also attack enemies. The user has explicitly confirmed tap attacks. Clicker Heroes supports the compatibility of active damage and automation, but the always-paid tap is Resonance's own decision. Targeting, area and attack limits remain separate design work; rhythm should not become a prerequisite for basic damage merely because the game is musical.
3. **Offer distinguishable economic and combat investments.** For example, one purchase improves Resonance yield; another develops an instrument's attack technique. Both may use the same currency. Their opportunity cost creates a useful question: grow faster or survive the next threat? If every upgrade improves both equally, that choice weakens. Musical naming should follow the mechanical effect, rather than force a new subsystem into existence.
4. **Keep discovery, deployment and development separate.** Awakening grants a being to inventory; arrangement places it; spending develops its performance. This preserves the user's recruitment direction without deleting the incremental economy. Initial placement costs are not settled by this recommendation. Paid rebuilding can remain distinct from recruitment, preserving learned development within a run as already agreed.
5. **Separate run and permanent progression conceptually before adding currencies.** A run budget and a persistent unlock/research layer have useful precedents. Their exact currencies and reset boundary should follow the run decision, not be introduced simply because other games have them. In particular, the researched examples do not establish whether discovered beings should remain in inventory after defeat.

## What makes the musical adaptation consequential

The conventional role of attack cadence can become rhythmic subdivision; attack character can become articulation; coordinated coverage can become ensemble relationships. These are possible mappings, not interchangeable upgrades: louder need not always mean better, and a new instrument should not automatically obsolete an earlier voice. Keep recognizable combat and economic consequences visible while allowing the resulting arrangement to be strange, dense and expressive.

The recommended starting distinction is **the orchestra earns; development spends; discovery recruits; arrangement connects; the conductor attacks and intervenes**. Production coming from performance is a recommendation awaiting the user's choice, not a conclusion the research can make for them.
