# Product brief

## Destination

Create a finished portrait Android game in which incremental mining and base construction, an automatic defensive orchestra, active conductor powers, and roguelite survival are distinct domains of one generative musical system.

The game should be complete without ads or recurring monetization. Burooj wants an optional one-time way to support it; whether that is an app purchase or a complete free app with an optional payment remains open.

## Settled direction

- The arena is top-down, radial, and pathless. Enemies approach a stationary central conductor from all directions.
- Instrument towers defend automatically. Their attacks are also musical performances, so the defensive arrangement creates the music.
- Tap, swipe, hold, and voice are conductor support. They are separate from instrument powers and remain useful during live play.
- Every ordinary tap earns resources. Global-beat accuracy may add a capped bonus but cannot gate base income.
- Mining and base building compound incrementally. Mines continue while the player is away; attacks and destruction do not.
- Ordinary play can be enjoyed without constant input. Bosses or crisis waves may make live support substantially important.
- Instruments have stable identities. Recruiting performers, arranging them, developing their technique, and unlocking parallel instruments are separate progression domains.
- Musical development may use tempo, pitch, register, beat division, phase, harmony, dynamics, articulation, or relationships within the arrangement.
- The sound may be intentionally dense and maximalist. Burooj likes the current prototype's musical direction.
- Runs escalate until defeat. Roguelite variation may alter conductor powers, arrangements, instrument techniques, or the environment for one run.
- The visual direction draws from EmotiTone's dark cut-paper stage, glowing blobs, vibrating strings, beat-responsive geometry, and selective color.

## Design frontier

The wayfinding map must resolve enough of these relationships to make a specification possible:

- The progression grammar: which musical changes are global, instrument-specific, arrangement-specific, run-specific, or permanent.
- The economy: resource fiction, compounding curves, mining expansion, active tap value, tempo effects, and spending tension.
- The orchestra: instrument roles, recruitment, placement, networking, damage, destruction, repair, and arrangement bonuses.
- The conductor: the support vocabulary for tap, swipe, hold, and voice; power limits; targeting; and roguelite variations.
- The opposition: enemy behaviors, wave escalation, crisis cadence, loss, recovery, and the boundary between idle safety and required presence.
- The run and metagame: what resets, what persists, what unlocks, and how a base can grow incrementally without erasing roguelite variation.
- The musical system: how increasing complexity remains legible, how global harmony is maintained, and how combat outcomes reshape the composition.
- The product boundary: Android technology, accessibility, offline behavior, payment structure, and the smallest finished release.

## Guardrails

- Treat the project as a finite app-creation effort, not a live-service commitment.
- The game is not a pure instrument, passive visualizer, conventional tower defense with unrelated music, or accuracy-gated rhythm game.
- The ladder `solo → pair → section → electric` is rejected. It conflates headcount, arrangement, development, and instrument identity.
- EmotiTone is a visual and interaction reference. Changes to the EmotiTone project require their own authorization.
- Prototype mechanics remain provisional until play or decision work settles them.
