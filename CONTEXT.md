# Resonance language

This glossary names the player-facing concepts for the single game. Update a term when the design settles a new meaning; leave unresolved work in Linear.

## Global beat

The shared rhythmic clock against which mining, instrument actions, touch timing, and musical development are measured. Systems may use different divisions, phases, or multiples of this reference.

## Resonance

The provisional name for the spendable resource earned actively through touch and automatically through gathering. Gathering awakens dormant musical potential in the world; spending supports restoration and recruitment.

## Dormant musical potential

The world's latent capacity for music, present at particular locations and awakened through gathering.

## Active touch

A conductor tap that always earns Resonance during play and may additionally support a nearby part of the orchestra. Rhythmic accuracy may add a capped bonus but is not required for income.

## Conductor power

A supportive intervention performed by tapping, swiping, holding, or using voice. It belongs to the conductor rather than to any instrument and remains distinct from automatic instrument actions.

## Instrument

A stable musical and defensive identity that performs automatically in the orchestra. Development can alter how it plays, but one instrument does not upgrade into a different instrument.

## Parallel instrument

A separately available instrument identity with its own sound and role. Acoustic and electric guitars are parallel instruments rather than stages of one upgrade ladder.

## Musical development

An improvement or specialization that changes an instrument's mastery, beat division, phase, register, pitch behavior, dynamics, articulation, or relationship to the arrangement without replacing its identity.

## Recruitment

Adding another independently present musician or instrument to the orchestra. Headcount is capacity, not a quality level of an existing instrument.

## Arrangement

The relationships among active instruments, including entrances, rhythmic positions, registers, doubling, harmony, and coordinated techniques.

## Orchestra

The complete defensive arrangement of instruments and their relationships across the arena. It attacks automatically and generates the run's music.

## Mine

The provisional name for a gathering structure placed at a site of dormant musical potential. It produces resources on a division of the global beat, including while the player is away without advancing danger.

## Crisis

A wave or boss encounter designed to benefit substantially from live conductor support. Ordinary offline absence never resolves or destroys the orchestra through a crisis.

## Run

One escalating survival performance in which the base and orchestra develop until the conductor falls. Permanent progression may unlock possibilities; the reset boundary remains unsettled.
